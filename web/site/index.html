Local and offline use

No account, telemetry, or learner-data transfer is used. Attempts stay in this browser origin and can be deleted from the level menu. This site asks the browser to cache the complete candidate for later offline use; browser storage can be cleared or evicted, so confirm the cache state before relying on it away from a connection.

Science and training limits

All values, timings, organisms, handling effects, and thresholds are fictional instructional data. ATP-associated light is a viability-related metabolic signal; it is not proof of cell death or mechanism. Level 2 fluorescence supports simulated GFP expression only under the shown conditions.

This is not a laboratory protocol, calibrated instrument model, clinical tool, proof of hands-on competence, or prediction of a real cancer response. Follow local SOPs, equipment manuals, biosafety requirements, and supervisor instructions for real work.
